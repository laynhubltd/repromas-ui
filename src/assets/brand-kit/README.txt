REPROMAS BRAND KIT
==================

Brand colors
------------
Deep purple (primary)   #2E1065
Royal purple            #4C1D95
Violet                  #6D28D9
Bright violet           #8B5CF6
Lilac                   #A78BFA
Pale lilac              #C4B5FD
Mist                    #DDD6FE
Shell (light surface)   #EDE9FE
Muted text              #7C6BA8

Typography
----------
Monogram letters: Georgia / serif, bold
Wordmark and body: DejaVu Sans / Arial, bold for headings

Folders
-------
01-logo      Icon marks. "transparent" = no background, "white" = white
             background, "for-dark-bg" = light shell version to place on
             dark surfaces. SVG (scalable) + PNG (1024px).
02-lockup    Horizontal and stacked logo + wordmark combinations.
03-favicon   favicon.svg, favicon.ico and PNG sizes 16-512 including
             apple-touch-icon (180).
04-banner    1500x500 web/social banner.
05-flyer     A4 flyer (1240x1754 px, 150 dpi). Contact details are
             placeholders - replace before printing.
06-apparel   T-shirt and cap mockups plus a transparent print-ready
             chest graphic (light version, for dark garments).

Usage rules
-----------
1. Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of the "R".
2. Use the dark mark on light backgrounds and the shell (light) mark on
   dark backgrounds. Never place the dark mark on deep purple.
3. Do not rotate further, recolor, or add effects to the mark.
